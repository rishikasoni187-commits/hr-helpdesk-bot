# Nimbus Analytics Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Handbook (Sample, v1.0)

*Fictional company and policies, created for a college project. Not real HR or legal guidance.*

## 1. Working Hours & Attendance
1.1 Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
1.2 Employees must be present in the core collaboration window of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM on working days.
1.3 Three late arrivals (after 10:00 AM) in a calendar month count as one half-day casual leave.

## 2. Leave Policy (Full-time employees)
2.1 **Casual Leave (CL):** 8 days per calendar year. Maximum 3 consecutive days at a time. Cannot be carried forward.
2.2 **Sick Leave (SL):** 10 days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than 2 consecutive days. Cannot be carried forward.
2.3 **Earned Leave (EL):** 18 days per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Up to 30 days can be carried forward to the next year. Applications for more than 5 consecutive days need 14 days' prior notice.
2.4 **Maternity Leave:** 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.
2.5 **Paternity Leave:** 5 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.
2.6 **Bereavement Leave:** 3 days of paid leave for the death of an immediate family member (parent, spouse, child, sibling).
2.7 Leave must be applied for through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager. Unapproved absence of 3 or more consecutive days may be treated as absconding.
2.8 Contract employees are entitled to 6 days of CL and 6 days of SL per year. They are not eligible for EL, maternity or paternity leave under this handbook.

## 3. Work From Home (WFH)
3.1 Full-time employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
3.2 Employees in their probation period (first 6 months) may work from home only in exceptional cases, with written approval from their manager and HR.
3.3 Employees working from home must be available on the company chat tool during working hours.

## 4. Probation & Confirmation
4.1 The probation period is 6 months from the date of joining.
4.2 Confirmation is based on a performance review by the reporting manager. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months.
4.3 During probation, either party may end employment with 15 days' written notice.

## 5. Notice Period & Exit
5.1 After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for all employees.
5.2 Notice period buy-out is possible only with written approval from the department head and HR.
5.3 Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
5.4 Unused Earned Leave (up to 30 days) is encashed at the time of exit, based on basic salary.

## 6. Reimbursements & Expenses
6.1 **Travel:** Client-related travel is reimbursed on actuals with bills. Economy class only for flights; 3-star hotels capped at Rs. 4,000 per night.
6.2 **Meals:** Rs. 500 per day during official travel. Meals during normal working days are not reimbursed.
6.3 **Internet allowance:** Employees on approved WFH arrangements get Rs. 800 per month as an internet allowance.
6.4 Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, through the HR portal, with original bills.
6.5 Claims are approved by the reporting manager and paid with the next salary cycle.

## 7. Payroll & Benefits
7.1 Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
7.2 Group health insurance of Rs. 5,00,000 covers the employee, spouse and up to 2 children from the date of confirmation.
7.3 Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 3rd of the following month.
7.4 Queries on tax declarations (Form 12BB) must be submitted by 15th January each year.

## 8. Code of Conduct & Workplace Safety
8.1 Nimbus Analytics has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind.
8.2 Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH Act, 2013. Complaints can be sent confidentially to ic@nimbus-sample.example.
8.3 Employees must not share confidential client data on personal email or unapproved tools.

## 9. Holidays
9.1 The company observes 12 paid public holidays per year. The list is published each January on the HR portal.
9.2 Employees may take up to 2 optional (floating) holidays from a published list.

## 10. Contact
10.1 For anything this handbook does not answer, contact HR at hr@nimbus-sample.example or raise a ticket through this assistant.
